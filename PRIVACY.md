# TraceGuard — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

TraceGuard is an Atlassian Forge app made by an independent developer (Shaon Bandyopadhyay) for Codegeist 2026.

**What TraceGuard reads.** When you use it, TraceGuard reads Jira issues (summary, labels, status, fix versions and
issue links) and Confluence spaces/pages that you are allowed to see, using your own permissions (`asUser`).

**What TraceGuard writes.** Only when you ask it to: the demo project (Jira project, version, issues and links) and
audit-report pages in Confluence.

**What TraceGuard stores.** Nothing. TraceGuard has no database and no storage of its own, keeps no copies of your
data, and does not store personal data such as account IDs. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform
("Runs on Atlassian") and sends no data to any external server.

**Logs.** Atlassian's Forge platform may keep standard app logs as described in Atlassian's own privacy policy.

**Contact.** Questions or requests: https://github.com/shaon5775/traceguard/issues
